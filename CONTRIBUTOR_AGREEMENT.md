## Terms and Conditions

### 1. Copyright Retention
1. The Contributor retains full moral and intellectual copyright in the Work. This Agreement constitutes an exclusive digital publication licence for the term specified herein; it does not constitute an assignment or transfer of copyright ownership.
2. The Contributor confirms that they own or legally control all rights necessary to execute this Agreement and have disclosed any prior publication, third-party permissions, or existing publishing commitments that may impact publication on Dendra Folio.

### 2. Licence Granted to Dendra Folio
1. The Contributor grants to Dendra Folio an irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free licence to publish, host, digitally display, distribute, archive, and promote the Work across Dendra Folio’s digital properties (including `dendrafolio.com`, authenticated reader libraries, and digital subscriber letters).
2. For a period of **twelve (12) consecutive months** commencing from the initial date of publication on Dendra Folio (the "Exclusive Period"), this licence is exclusive with respect to other online magazines, digital periodicals, web journals, and comparable online editorial publishers, subject only to the specific exceptions enumerated in Section 3.
3. Dendra Folio is authorized to make reasonable proofreading, typography formatting, syntax adjustments, and digital accessibility adaptations that preserve the essential meaning, argument, and creative voice of the Work.
4. Dendra Folio is authorized to reproduce the Work’s title, agreed byline, biographical note, and brief excerpts in promotional materials, social communications, and editorial indexes.
5. This Agreement does not authorize Dendra Folio to sell the Work to third-party publishers, license the copyright to commercial entities, or include the Work in paid commercial anthologies without separate written agreement and agreed compensation.

### 3. Exclusivity & Permitted Author Uses During the First Twelve Months
1. During the twelve-month Exclusive Period, the Contributor agrees not to pitch, submit, license, or publish the Work with another online magazine, digital journal, or competitive commercial web publication.
2. **Permitted Carve-Outs:** The exclusivity restriction does **not** restrict the Contributor from:
   * Publishing the Work in a print collection, print anthology, physical chapbook, or standalone printed book, provided such offline publication does not grant conflicting online rights.
   * Sharing brief excerpts, quotes, or promotional links across the Contributor’s personal social media accounts.
   * Publishing the full Work on the Contributor’s personal website, Substack, or personal blog following initial publication on Dendra Folio, provided clear and prominent attribution is included:  
     *“First published by Dendra Folio (dendrafolio.com).”*
   * Discussing, performing, or presenting the Work at public readings, academic lectures, or literary events.

### 4. Simultaneous Submissions & Prior Acceptance
1. If the Work was submitted simultaneously to other publishers prior to entering this Agreement, the Contributor must disclose this status to Dendra Folio.
2. If another publisher accepts the Work prior to formal acceptance and publication scheduling by Dendra Folio, the Contributor must immediately notify Dendra Folio in writing, whereupon the Work will be withdrawn from the publication queue with no remaining obligation on either party.
3. Once this Agreement is executed and the piece is scheduled for publication, the Contributor must not enter into conflicting agreements that would impair Dendra Folio's agreed Exclusive Period.

### 5. Rights & Requests Following the Twelve-Month Period
1. Following the expiration of the twelve-month Exclusive Period, the Contributor may request in writing release from online exclusivity in order to pitch or publish the Work with another online publication.
2. Dendra Folio will consider all such requests in good faith, balancing the Contributor's career opportunities with Dendra Folio's legitimate archival integrity and reader library commitments.
3. **Release is not automatic.** Mutual release requires written confirmation from Dendra Folio. Upon formal release, Dendra Folio may retain a permanent digital archival record or tombstone notice in its master register while withdrawing the public essay page as agreed.
4. The Contributor must obtain written release before representing the Work to any third party as available for first or exclusive publication.

### 6. Withdrawal Prior to Twelve Months
1. The Contributor agrees not to seek removal during the first twelve months on arbitrary grounds (such as receiving a later offer or changing personal preference).
2. Dendra Folio will nevertheless consider withdrawal requests in good faith at any time where compelling circumstances exist, including:
   * Genuine, credible safety or personal security concerns.
   * Legitimate, substantiated third-party intellectual property claims.
   * Unresolvable factual inaccuracies that compromise journalistic integrity.
3. Dendra Folio reserves the reciprocal right to correct, archive, or unpublish any Work at its sole editorial discretion where legal, copyright, or ethical concerns arise.
4. Where a piece is withdrawn, cached search engine results, delivered email newsletters, and private offline backups may not be immediately retractable. The Article ID will be marked as `Retired` in the Master Article Register and will never be reassigned.

### 7. Editorial Process & Author Attribution
1. Dendra Folio exercises careful editorial stewardship. Line edits or structural suggestions that materially alter the argument or creative core will be discussed with the Contributor prior to final publication.
2. Dendra Folio will prominently display the agreed byline, biographical note, and publication date on the published piece and maintain authorial credit across digital distributions.

### 8. Warranties of Originality & Permissions
1. The Contributor expressly warrants that:
   * The Work is their own original creation and does not infringe upon any copyright, trademark, patent, or proprietary right of any third party.
   * The Work does not contain defamatory, libelous, or unlawful material.
   * The Contributor has secured written permission for any copyrighted third-party material (such as extensive song lyrics, protected poetry, or unpublished letters) reproduced in the Work.
   * Any material use of artificial intelligence tools in research, translation, or outlining has been fully disclosed in accordance with the Dendra Folio AI Policy.
2. The Contributor agrees to indemnify and hold harmless Dendra Folio and its operators against reasonable legal expenses and damages arising from a proven breach of these originality warranties.

### 9. AI Policy & Manuscript Confidentiality
1. Both parties agree to adhere strictly to the **Dendra Folio AI Policy**:
   * The Contributor warrants that the literary voice, creative prose, and philosophical arguments are authentic human creations, not synthetic text generated by artificial intelligence.
   * Dendra Folio covenants that it will **never upload** the Contributor's unpublished manuscript, draft revisions, or private editorial correspondence to external public or third-party AI models without explicit written consent.
   * Neither party will disclose confidential editorial communications without mutual consent.

### 10. Financial Terms
1. Unless explicitly agreed otherwise in a separate written Commission Addendum signed by both parties, publication on Dendra Folio is non-commercial, honorary literary exhibition, and no licensing fee or royalty is payable.
2. The voluntary **Support Dendra Folio** reader patronage fund is dedicated to general website hosting, digital infrastructure, and editorial maintenance. It does not establish a royalty entitlement for individual contributors unless a specific writer-share arrangement has been executed in writing.

### 11. Moral Rights & Integrity
1. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as an assignment of the Contributor's moral rights under Section 57 of the Indian Copyright Act, 1957.
2. The Contributor retains the right to claim authorship and object to any distortion, mutilation, or modification prejudicial to their authorial honor or reputation.

### 12. Governing Law & Jurisdiction
1. This Agreement is executed and shall be governed by, and interpreted in accordance with, the laws of the Republic of India.
2. The parties agree to resolve any dispute or misunderstanding through amicable, good-faith dialogue. If a dispute cannot be resolved within thirty (30) days of written notice, it shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in `[City/Jurisdiction, India]`.

### 13. Entire Agreement
1. This Agreement, together with the Dendra Folio Editorial Policy, AI Policy, and Master Article Register entry, constitutes the complete understanding between the parties regarding the Work, superseding all prior oral or informal discussions.
2. Modifications must be executed in writing and signed or formally recorded by both parties.
3. Execution via electronic signature, recorded email confirmation, or verifiable digital agreement is valid and legally binding.
