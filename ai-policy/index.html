AI POLICY

"Human thought. Human voice. Responsible tools."

Last updated: October 2026

Our Foundational Principle

Dendra Folio exists to cultivate distinctive human writing, disciplined philosophical inquiry, and authentic artistic expression. We believe that enduring literature springs from human consciousness, that is from memory, moral struggle, spiritual intuition, and lived experience.

While computational tools continue to advance, we maintain that using a digital instrument must never substitute for creative authorship, critical judgment, or intellectual honesty.

Guidelines for Contributors

1. What Is Permitted

We welcome digital tools that assist the writing and editing process without supplanting authorial intent:

  • Conventional spellcheckers and typographical formatting tools.
  • Standard digital dictionaries, thesauri, and encyclopedic reference indexes.
  • Accessibility adaptations (such as text-to-speech proof-listening).

Writers remain entirely responsible for the finished text, including verifying any stylistic or grammatical changes recommended by an automated tool.

2. What Requires Transparent Disclosure

We require contributors to disclose upon submission if generative AI was materially utilized during the creation of a piece for:

  • Brainstorming broad structural outlines or contrasting arguments.
  • Summarizing complex technical, legal, or academic research documents.
  • Assisting with rough translation drafts from original source languages.
  • Generating developmental feedback or exploring phrasing alternatives.

Disclosure does not automatically disqualify a submission. Our editorial desk evaluates the nature and extent of the assistance to ensure the finished piece firmly embodies the author's own intellectual, artistic, and philosophical voice.

3. What We Do Not Accept

Absolute Boundary: We do not publish essays, poetry, or fiction substantially written, generated, or rewritten by artificial intelligence and submitted as the contributor's original literary or intellectual creation.

In addition, we strictly reject:

  • Fabricated quotations, hallucinated citations, synthetic interviews, or simulated personal memoirs presented as factual.
  • Material that infringes upon third-party copyrights or incorporates protected works without lawful authorization.

Every author bears full legal and moral responsibility for factual veracity, permissions, and ethical citation.

Mutual Covenants: Protecting Unpublished Manuscripts

We treat the creative sanctuary of our writers with utmost seriousness:

  • Author Protection Covenant: Contributors must not upload confidential editorial correspondence, private editorial feedback, or unpublished Dendra Folio manuscripts into public or third-party AI models where training ingestion, automated data retention, or data scraping may occur.
  • Publisher Protection Covenant: Dendra Folio covenants that it never uploads an author's unpublished manuscript, draft, or private correspondence to external AI systems for automated rewriting, line editing, or algorithmic evaluation without express written permission.

How We Evaluate Concerns

We recognize that commercial "AI detection" software is inherently probabilistic, frequently produces false positives, and cannot serve as definitive evidence of misconduct. We do not treat automated detection scores as conclusive proof.

If a credible concern arises regarding authenticity, our editors initiate a direct, collegial dialogue with the author to discuss their creative process, draft evolution, and research notes. Decisions remain grounded in human discernment and fair review.

Where intentional deception or wholesale automated generation is established, Dendra Folio declines the submission, rescinds acceptance, or unpublishes the piece immediately.

Our Editorial Commitment to Readers

Our readers come to Dendra Folio seeking authentic contemplation. We pledge that:

  • All editorial letters, essays, poems, and fiction published on Dendra Folio represent genuine human craft.
  • We never publish AI-generated synthetic articles disguised as human reflection.

Editorial Disclosures & Questions

Contributors wishing to disclose the material use of assistive research or translation tools in their writing process may do so directly in their submission cover email to our editorial desk.
